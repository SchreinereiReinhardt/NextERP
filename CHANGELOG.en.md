## 2.4.93

### The biggest Betrio update so far

With this release, I am making a large part of the functionality that I have been using and developing internally in Betrio for quite some time available to the community.

The focus was not on adding as many isolated features as possible, but on connecting the existing areas into a consistent workflow. Quotations, orders, projects, materials, working time, reports, suppliers, invoices, payments, project costing and DATEV now work much more closely together.

### New or significantly expanded

- Project controlling and actual/target costing with internal personnel, material and external costs
- Purchasing, supplier cockpit and project-related incoming documents
- Warehouse withdrawals as actual project costs and transfer to billing
- Billing assistant for working time, materials and services not yet invoiced
- Connected document chain from quotation and order to delivery note and invoice
- Invoice audit trail, finalization, cancellations and credit notes
- DATEV EXTF export and expanded finance views
- Labour-cost share according to German § 35a EStG
- Smart Document Inbox with project, customer and supplier assignment
- Revised project notes, help and settings
- Numerous usability and workflow improvements

Many of these functions grew out of daily use of Betrio. With this release they are now available to all Betrio users.
